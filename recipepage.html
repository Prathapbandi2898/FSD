Chocalate Cake

A simple and delicious chocolate cake recipe for beginners.

perfect for birthdays and celebrations!

Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1tsp cinnamon(potional)
  • 1/2 cup cococa powder
  • 1 cup milk

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180C.
  2. Slowly mix the flour,sugar,and cococa powder.
  3. Add eggs and milk,stir until smooth.
  4. Bake for 30 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.

Tips

for extra flavour,add chocolate chips before baking.

You can also server with vanilla ice cream for a delicious combo!
